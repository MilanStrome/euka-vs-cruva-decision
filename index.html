What we don't actually know

Read this before the team locks in a decision off this memo alone.

Thin proof

Euka has 2 public G2 reviews (one marked "incentivized"). We found no populated G2 or Trustpilot profile for Cruva at all.

Unverified

Both companies' "4M+ creators" and total GMV figures are self-reported and not independently auditable.

Zero niche proof

No published case study for either tool in kids' / parenting / Montessori / toy categories. Public case studies skew beauty and wellness.

No real Reddit data

Several "Reddit review" articles we found are SEO content, not actual Reddit threads. Genuine seller-community sentiment is largely unverified.

Recommendation
